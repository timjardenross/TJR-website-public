## Find a consultation

The Department of Health runs a [consultation hub](https://consultations.health.gov.au/) that lists open consultations. Check each one's closing date, and whether it relates to pain or chronic conditions.

## Tips

- Read the questions first, and answer the question asked.
- Keep your own story short. One or two sentences about how this affects you can be enough.
- Check whether your submission will be published, and what choices you have about that.
- Ask for what you want to happen.

## Template

Copy this and change it to suit you. The words in square brackets are for you to fill in.

### Who I am

[Your name, or say you prefer to stay anonymous. A sentence on your connection to the issue, for example that you live with persistent pain or care for someone who does.]

### My interest

[Why this consultation matters to you.]

### My answer

[Your answer to the question asked.]

### My ask

[What you would like to see change, in one or two sentences.]

### Publication preference

[Say whether you are happy for your submission to be published, and under what name.]
