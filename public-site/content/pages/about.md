## Who I am

I'm Tim. I have lived with chronic pain for most of my life, and I work in operational resilience in a corporate role. I do this advocacy alongside that work.

My aim is simple: help people with persistent pain be heard, be better prepared to speak up, and be better understood by the people around them.

You can read [my story](/my-story), and see [how stories are handled](/story-and-consent) on this site.

---

## Who I am not

- I am not a clinician, and nothing here is medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
- I am not an official body. I speak from lived experience, as an individual.
- I am not Painaustralia. [Painaustralia](https://www.painaustralia.org.au/) is the national peak body for pain in Australia, and it is a good place to learn more and to find ways to take part in national advocacy.

---

## What you will find here

- **A practical guide for people living with persistent pain.** The [Persistent Pain Pathway](/pain/understand) helps you understand your pain, find the right help, manage your capacity, and prepare for flares.
- **Tools for speaking up.** Over time, this site will add more help for preparing for appointments and being heard.
- **Ways to take action.** Over time, you will be able to find simple ways to support awareness and advocacy.

If you would like to share your experience, volunteer, or work together, [get involved](/get-involved).
