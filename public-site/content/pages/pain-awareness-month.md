## September

September is International Pain Awareness Month. In Australia, Painaustralia, the national peak body for pain, runs the Wear Red for Pain campaign. In 2026 it was on 1 September. Check [Painaustralia](https://www.painaustralia.org.au/) for this year's date and details.

## How to take part

Painaustralia encourages Australians, workplaces, health professionals, community pharmacies and organisations to wear red on the day. The aim is to help make chronic pain more visible, and to show support for people living with chronic pain.

You can also:

- Share the [pain facts](/pain-facts), with their sources and dates.
- [Get involved](/get-involved) if you would like to share your experience or help out.
