## Whose stories are here

Right now, the only story on this site is [Tim's own](/my-story). It is his experience, not advice. It was written in November 2026 and is reviewed each year.

## Visitor stories are not open yet

Nothing you send through the [Get involved](/get-involved) form is published. If visitor stories open later, these rules will apply first.

## If stories open

- **You choose how you are identified:** your full name, your first name only, or anonymously.
- **You can change your mind** at any time and ask for your story to be removed.
- **Nothing is published without your review.** You see the final version before anyone else does.
- **Your story is never used for fundraising** without your explicit permission.
- **Stories are treated with dignity.** They are not edited to sensationalise, and every story carries a content note.

## Support

If a story brings up something difficult, you can call Lifeline on 13 11 14 or Beyond Blue on 1300 22 4636.

## Privacy

Stories and the details people send are never stored in this website's code repository. See the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) for how information is handled.
