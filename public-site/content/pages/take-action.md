## Four ways to help

- **Write to your MP.** [Use the letter template](/letter-to-your-mp).
- **Have your say.** [Use the consultation template](/consultation-submission).
- **Share the facts.** [Read and share the pain facts](/pain-facts).
- **Join Pain Awareness Month.** [See how September works](/pain-awareness-month).

## Not sure where to start?

Start small: share one fact, or send one short letter. A few honest sentences are enough.

## Who else is doing this

[Painaustralia](https://www.painaustralia.org.au/) is the national peak body for pain in Australia. This site is an individual voice, not an official body, and does not speak for Painaustralia.
