## The facts

- Painaustralia's 2026 awareness campaign says chronic pain affects 4.2 million adults in Australia. Estimates vary by year and by how they are counted, so treat this as Painaustralia's current figure. Source: [Painaustralia media release, 31 August 2026](https://www.painaustralia.org.au/media/mr-2026/the-pain-you-cant-see).
- Chronic pain is often invisible: many people manage its impact every day without those around them knowing. Source: [Painaustralia media release, 31 August 2026](https://www.painaustralia.org.au/media/mr-2026/the-pain-you-cant-see).
- Australia has a National Strategic Action Plan for Pain Management. Source: [Department of Health, National Strategic Action Plan for Pain Management](https://www.health.gov.au/resources/publications/the-national-strategic-action-plan-for-pain-management).
