## Get involved

If you live with persistent pain, support someone who does, or want to help improve how pain is understood, I would like to hear from you.

You can:

- Share your experience.
- Volunteer or help out.
- Collaborate or partner on awareness and advocacy.
- Ask about media or speaking.

Email **[support@tjrmindbody.com](mailto:support@tjrmindbody.com)**, or use the form on this page.

We aim to reply within 2 business days.

This is an individual voice, not an official body or a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
