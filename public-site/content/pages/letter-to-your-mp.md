## Find your MP

Search by name or electorate on the [Parliament of Australia's Senators and Members search](https://www.aph.gov.au/Senators_and_Members/Parliamentarian_Search_Results).

## Tips

- Keep it to one page.
- Say your suburb, so the office knows you are a constituent.
- Share only the personal health detail you are comfortable with.
- Ask for something specific, and ask for a reply.

## Template

Copy this and change it to suit you. The words in square brackets are for you to fill in.

Dear [MP's name],

I am writing as a constituent in [your suburb].

[One or two sentences about how pain affects your life. Share only what you are comfortable with.]

Chronic pain affects an estimated 4.2 million adults in Australia (Painaustralia, [media release, 31 August 2026](https://www.painaustralia.org.au/media/mr-2026/the-pain-you-cant-see)).

I am asking you to support:

1. timely, affordable access to multidisciplinary pain care;
2. greater awareness of chronic pain; and
3. research into pain and new treatments.

I would welcome your support for national action on these, including the [National Strategic Action Plan for Pain Management](https://www.health.gov.au/resources/publications/the-national-strategic-action-plan-for-pain-management).

I would welcome a reply, or a meeting, to talk about this.

Yours sincerely,

[your name]
[your suburb and postcode]
