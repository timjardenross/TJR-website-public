## Talked about, not talked with

My pain began when I was 12. Years of appointments, surgeries and hospital stays followed. Being a patient became familiar. Being heard didn't.

I remember staff discussing my case without including me. I remember being labelled "weak" at school because of my scoliosis. And I still find it hard to speak up in the medical system, for fear of being dismissed.

If you live with persistent pain, you may know this feeling. It isn't a sign that you're difficult, or that you're doing it wrong. Pain that lasts is hard to explain, and appointments are short.

## What I'm doing about it

This site is where I'm putting together what I wish I'd had: ways to prepare for an appointment, put your own story into words, and ask for what matters to you.

- Start with [how to be heard in an appointment](/how-to-be-heard).
- Read [my story](/my-story) if you'd like to know where this comes from.
- If you'd like to share your experience or help out, [get involved](/get-involved).

You're not alone in this.

*This is my experience only. It isn't medical advice, and your own care team can help you work out what's right for you.*
